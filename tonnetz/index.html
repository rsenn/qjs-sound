TONNETZ TABLE

Touch a triangle on the tonnetz (or a node, or the line between two nodes) and slide across it to play chords on a distorted FM electric piano.

Whatever you leave latched becomes the starting point: an invisible arpeggiator keeps churning out a voice-led chord progression from it, plays the chords on the piano and arpeggiates them on an acid VCO/VCF voice. It resolves back to your chord at the end of every phrase. Dashed triangles show where it may go next.

Switch Mode to direct for no sequencer at all: exactly the notes you touch sound while your finger is down.

Space pauses. To patch: tap a module, then the module (or OUT) it should feed. Tap the x on a cable to disconnect. Each ring around a module is one parameter: the knob follows your finger's angle. Drag the middle to move it, double-tap to remove it.
